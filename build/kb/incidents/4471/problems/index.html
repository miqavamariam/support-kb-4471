The three problems

parser: the shared parser read dates month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so “7 April” came out as “4 July”. This is the bug #4471 named. Fixed on 5 October.

hint: the booking-v3 form told people to type the month first. Its date field printed MM/DD/YYYY, but the form read what people typed the tenant’s way, day first. People did what the hint said and the date flipped. Same symptom as parser, different code: the parser fix did not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so the hint is gone with it.

move: the booking lands one day off. Day and month are not swapped. The same meeting shows on different days in Lisbon and Toronto, and one tenant sees it only in June. That points at time zones, not at date order, so neither change on 5 October covers it. The desk read these as a one-day move and counts them under parser by tenant, so they had no ticket. Open.

How we told them apart

  • By the tenant (its locale and booking form, locales.ts) and the channel, not by intake’s tag. Intake tags by the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the tenant often: it tags en-US browsers as hint, which catches about half of the real hint reports and mislabels a set of parser reports.
  • By what the dates in the report do: a swap (day ↔ month) or a one-day move.
  • Counted the way the desk’s own view counts them, from the export of 4 October. Duplicate rows count once, by report id.

Lessons

  • Matching on subject (decision 0001) merges by symptom. Different bugs with the same symptom end up in one ticket, and closing it closes all of them.
  • Before a big ticket is marked known or closed, check its reports against the cause, not the subject.
